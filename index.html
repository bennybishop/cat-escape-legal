You can play without creating an account. Progress is saved on your device. The game also includes third-party analytics and advertising technology, which can process device identifiers and information about how the game is used. No account does not mean no data collection.

1. Scope and contact

This policy applies to the mobile game Cat Escape!, also identified in earlier builds as Cat Gunner Run, with Android package com.catgunnerrun.game. The developer and privacy contact is Benjamin Dehant, reachable at benjamin.dehant@gmail.com.

The game integrates YSO Corp GameUtils and the services described below. Those providers may process information on behalf of the game operator or for their own purposes, as described in their policies and applicable arrangements. Contact us if you need help identifying the provider responsible for a request.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your level, virtual currency, upgrades, equipped items, tutorial status, language and sound preferences locally. SDKs may also store identifiers, session counters, attribution information and privacy preferences. These local files are separate from information sent to service providers. Android backup or device-transfer features may copy application data depending on your device settings.

3. Information processed by online services

  • Device and other identifiers: device-derived identifiers, advertising identifiers when available, and session identifiers. Some providers can associate these with installation or attribution information.
  • Game activity: launches, session duration, levels started, completed or lost, pauses and other gameplay events. Advertising services, when active, can process ad views, clicks and reward events.
  • Technical information: device model, operating system, app and SDK versions, memory and processor characteristics, loading times and performance measurements.
  • Network and approximate location: online providers receive your IP address and may derive a general country, city or region from it. The current Android build does not request precise or approximate GPS location permission.
  • Advertising and attribution information, when the relevant service is active: install source, campaigns, referral data, ad network, ad placement and impression revenue. Included advertising SDKs may check whether particular apps or URL handlers are available on the device.
  • Support correspondence: if you email us, we receive the address and information you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or unnecessary identity documents.

The game does not ask you to enter a name, email address, phone number or payment card to play. The current game does not offer account creation, social login or real-money in-app purchases. Its shop uses virtual game currency. Its Android build does not request camera, microphone, contacts, SMS or location permissions. Permission absence does not prevent IP-derived location or SDK telemetry.

4. Why information is processed

Information is used to operate and improve the game, understand gameplay and performance, measure installations and advertising effectiveness, deliver or personalize advertising when enabled and permitted, detect fraud and technical problems, and respond to support or privacy requests. The processing that occurs depends on the game version, provider configuration, region, device settings and applicable choices.

Where consent is required for advertising, tracking or device access, it is the applicable basis for that processing. Other processing may be necessary to provide a requested service, meet legal obligations or pursue legitimate interests such as security, subject to applicable law. You may contact us to ask which basis applies or to withdraw consent or object where applicable. This page is an information notice, not a consent dialog.

5. SDKs, advertising partners and sharing

The game includes YSO Corp GameUtils for analytics and integration, Meta/Facebook SDK for app events and measurement, AppLovin MAX for advertising mediation, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for supported consent flows, and Tenjin for attribution and measurement.

Presence in the application does not mean every service is active on every launch. In Android build 1.0.6, the configured Android ad unit IDs are empty, so the normal GameUtils path does not initialize MAX and its dependent Tenjin activation. YSO analytics and Facebook have separate initialization paths. Later configured releases may activate advertising services; this policy covers those integrations as well.

The included mediation integrations are Adjoe Ads/Wave, BidMachine, BIGO Ads, Pangle, Meta Audience Network, Digital Turbine/Fyber, InMobi, ironSource, LINE, Mintegral, MobileFuse, Moloco, Ogury, Unity Ads, Verve, Liftoff Monetize/Vungle, Yandex, YSO Network and Voodoo ADN. Which partners actually process an ad request depends on configuration and applicable choices.

Information may be transferred to these providers for analytics, attribution and advertising, and to technical providers acting for them. Advertising providers may use information for their own measurement and advertising purposes. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or in a business transfer subject to applicable protections.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains until you reset progress or remove application data, subject to backups and device transfers. Reset progress affects the game save and is not a request to erase SDK records. YSO Corp states a maximum personal-data retention period of 13 months after collection, followed where applicable by anonymization. Other providers apply their own retention schedules; this is not a universal 13-month limit for every partner.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and applicable legal requirements. To request access, correction or deletion of information associated with the game, follow our data deletion and rights instructions. Requests involving service providers require coordination and sufficient information to locate the relevant records; legal or security retention exceptions may apply.

8. Security and international processing

The game uses HTTPS for its YSO analytics endpoint. Service providers apply their respective security measures. Information may be processed outside your country, including in Europe and the United States, under the providers' applicable arrangements and safeguards. We do not promise that all partners store information in one country or that all stored data is encrypted. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.
