Cat Escape!
Request data deletion

Last updated: October 18, 2026 · Android package com.catgunnerrun.game

No game account is required. You can still request deletion of information associated with analytics or other online services.

1. Submit your request

Email Benjamin Dehant at benjamin.dehant@gmail.com with the subject Cat Escape! — data deletion request.

  • State whether you want deletion, access, correction or help withdrawing consent.
  • Include the game name, Android version, approximate dates of use, and app version if known.
  • If you already know a relevant provider or device/ad identifier, you may include it privately so we can locate records. If not, contact us first for help. Do not publish identifiers on GitHub.

No login is required to send a request. Do not send passwords, payment card details or identity documents unless a specific, proportionate verification process is subsequently explained.

2. What the request covers

We will review your request and coordinate as needed with YSO Corp and the applicable analytics or advertising providers. Relevant records may include device or session identifiers, gameplay/session events and advertising/attribution records. Providers may need additional information to identify records. Deletion is not instantaneous, and some records may be retained where required for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons; applicable exceptions will be explained when handling the request.

YSO Corp can also be contacted at contact@ysocorp.com under its privacy policy. Tenjin directs end-user requests to the app operator. Do not assume that contacting one provider deletes records held independently by every other provider.

3. Remove local progress

Use the game's reset-progress option to erase the game save. Android's application settings also offer a clear-storage option. Removing local data can erase progress, virtual currency, upgrades and preferences. Device or cloud backups may retain copies according to your Android settings. These actions do not erase previously transmitted data held by service providers.

4. Retention

YSO's published policy states up to 13 months for personal data after collection. Other providers use their own retention schedules. We retain request correspondence as needed to process it and meet legal obligations. Contact us for information about the specific provider and records involved.
